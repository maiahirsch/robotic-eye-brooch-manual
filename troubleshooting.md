# Troubleshooting

[← Back to Manual](index.md)

## Brooch Does Not Turn On

Check:

1. Battery charge
2. Power cable connections
3. Raspberry Pi power
4. Controller connection
5. Battery orientation

---

## One or Both Servos Do Not Move

Check:

- Servo cable connection
- Controller connection
- Mechanical obstruction
- Loose servo horn
- Loose screw
- Power supply

---

## Eye Moves but Does Not Track

Check:

- Tracking Mode is active
- Two LEDs are on
- Camera is connected
- Raspberry Pi is powered
- Tracking software is running

Try restarting the Raspberry Pi.

---

## Tracking Stops Working

The Raspberry Pi may have overheated.

Power the system down and allow it to cool.

Do not continuously run the tracking configuration for more than approximately 5 minutes.

---

## LED Does Not Turn On

Check:

- LED connection
- Polarity
- Controller wiring
- Replacement LED

Replacement LEDs are included in the kit.

---

## Eye Is Mechanically Stuck

Check for:

- Loose screws
- Obstruction between the eye and housing
- Misaligned servo horn
- Decorative PLA interfering with movement

Do not force the eye manually.

---
