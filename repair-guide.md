# Repair Guide

[← Back to Manual](index.md)

Always fully power off and disconnect the brooch before performing repairs.

---

## Replacing a Servo

1. Turn off the brooch.
2. Disconnect the batteries.
3. Remove the rear or decorative housing as necessary.
4. Photograph the existing wiring before disconnecting anything.
5. Disconnect the damaged servo.
6. Remove the mounting screws.
7. Install the replacement servo.
8. Reconnect the servo.
9. Test movement before fully reassembling the housing.

---

## Replacing an LED

1. Power off the system.
2. Disconnect the controller.
3. Identify the damaged LED.
4. Compare polarity and wiring before removal.
5. Install the replacement LED.
6. Test before reassembly.

Replacement LEDs are included in the kit.

---

## Replacing a Screw

The brooch uses:

- M2 × 4 mm
- M2 × 6 mm

Do not substitute screw lengths without checking the screw map.

[View screw placement →](mechanical-construction.md)

---

## Replacing a Brooch Attachment Pin

Replacement attachment pins are included.

1. Remove the damaged pin.
2. Inspect the PLA mounting area for damage.
3. Install the replacement pin.
4. Verify it is secure before attaching the brooch to clothing.

---

## Repairing a Printed Part

If a PLA part cracks or breaks:

1. Stop using the brooch.
2. Remove the damaged component.
3. Contact Maia Hirsch for the replacement STL file.
4. Reprint the part in PLA.
5. Transfer any heat-set inserts if appropriate.
6. Reassemble using the original hardware.

---

## Complex Repairs

If damage involves:

- Raspberry Pi
- Power electronics
- Camera
- Multiple broken wires
- Heat-set insert failure
- Structural damage

Contact Maia Hirsch before continuing.
