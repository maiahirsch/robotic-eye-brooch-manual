# Mechanical Construction

[← Back to Manual](index.md)

The brooch is primarily constructed from 3D printed PLA components.

---

## Main Body

The structural body is printed in black PLA.

It supports:

- Servo motors
- Eye mechanism
- Push button
- Electrical connections
- Decorative shell

---

## Decorative Components

The decorative outer pieces are printed in gold PLA.

These parts are primarily cosmetic and should be handled carefully during disassembly.

---

## Eye

The eye is made from PLA.

Finishing process:

1. 3D printed
2. Painted with acrylic paint
3. Covered with resin

Avoid scratching or applying solvents to the eye surface.

---

## Servo Mechanism

Two servos are mounted inside the body.

### Eye Movement Servo

Controls horizontal movement.

### Eyelid Servo

Controls blinking.

![Servo Layout](assets/images/servo-layout.png)

---

## Fasteners

All screws connect to heat-set inserts installed in the PLA parts.

The brooch uses:

- M2 × 4 mm screws
- M2 × 6 mm screws

Refer to the screw map before reinstalling any screw.
