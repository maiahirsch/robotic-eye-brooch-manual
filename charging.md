# Charging & Power

[← Back to Manual](index.md)

The brooch uses two different power configurations depending on how it is operated.

---

## Interactive Configuration Power

The Interactive Configuration uses:

- Samsung battery cells
- Geekworm X1205 power board
- Raspberry Pi 5
- Interactive Controller

The power chain is:

```text
Samsung Batteries
        ↓
Geekworm X1205
        ↓
Raspberry Pi 5
        ↓
Interactive Controller
        ↓
Brooch Electronics
