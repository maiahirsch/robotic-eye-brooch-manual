# Operating the Brooch

[← Back to Manual](index.md)

---

## Power On

1. Turn on the Raspberry Pi power system.
2. Allow the Raspberry Pi to boot.
3. Wait for the brooch to become ready.

![Power system](assets/images/power-on.jpg)

---

## Random Mode

Press the control button once.

In Random Mode:

- One indicator LED is illuminated.
- The camera is not used.
- The eye moves randomly.
- The eyelid blinks automatically.

![Random Mode](assets/images/random-mode.jpg)

---

## Tracking Mode

Press the button again.

In Tracking Mode:

- Two indicator LEDs are illuminated.
- The camera is active.
- The Raspberry Pi detects a hand.
- The eye follows the detected hand.

![Tracking Mode](assets/images/tracking-mode.jpg)

---

## Off Mode

Press the button a third time.

Both indicator LEDs should turn off.
