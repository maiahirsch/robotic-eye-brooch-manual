## Before Reconnecting Wires

1. Power off the entire system.
2. Disconnect all batteries.
3. Confirm wire orientation.
4. Compare the connection with the wiring diagram.
5. Do not force connectors.

If unsure, contact Maia Hirsch before reconnecting the system.
