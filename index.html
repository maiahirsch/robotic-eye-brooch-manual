Robotic Eye Brooch User Manual

Created by maiahirsch

Overview

Welcome to the official documentation for the Robotic Eye Brooch. This guide will walk you through the components, assembly, and operation of your interactive wearable device.

Required Materials & Tools

  • Brooch Hardware: 3D printed casing, safety pin back.
  • Electronics: Microcontroller (e.g., Seeed Studio XIAO), micro servo motor, 0.96" OLED screen or LED matrix.
  • Power: Small LiPo battery or coin cell setup.

Assembly Instructions

Step 1: Wiring the components together following the circuit diagram.
Step 2: Flashing the software code to the microcontroller using Arduino IDE.
Step 3: Securing the electronics snugly inside the brooch housing.

Troubleshooting

If the eye is jittery or not turning on, double-check your solder joints and ensure the battery is fully charged to 3.7V.
